Common questions

Where do the brew-time guidelines come from?

From specialty coffee references — SCA standards, James Hoffmann's published guides, and common pour-over practices. They're starting points; your beans, grind, and water will shift the actual brew.

Can I add custom ratios?

Custom ratios are planned for v1.1. v1 ships the most common ones (1:13 to 1:18 plus espresso 1:1.5 to 1:3).

Brew timer integration?

On the roadmap as a sibling app, BrewCalc Timer.
